1. Overview

Purpose of the Contest

This contest aims to share the enjoyment of competitive mathematics with HiGA students.
Unlike the mathematics learned in regular classes, competitive mathematics requires flexible thinking, creativity, and the use of various formulas and ideas to solve problems.
Through this contest, we hope that HiGA students will discover new aspects of mathematics and deepen their interest in the subject.

Who Can Participate

This contest is open to all HiGA students. Students who are already interested in mathematics are welcome, and students who want to start enjoying mathematics are also encouraged to participate.

2. Rules

Problem Categories

The contest problems will be selected from the following four common areas of competitive mathematics.

  • Algebra
  • Combinatorics
  • Geometry
  • Number Theory

Problems of various difficulty levels will be prepared for each category.
There will be 10 problems in each category, for a total of 40 problems.
The formulas and theorems needed to solve the problems are summarized in the Practice Pack, which will be distributed in advance.

Contest Format

This contest is an individual competition held on this website.
Participants may submit answers at any time during the contest, and their answers will be graded in real time.
After the contest ends, participants’ scores will be calculated and the results will be displayed on the ranking table.

Scoring System

Scores are added based on the difficulty of the problems answered correctly.
The difficulty of each problem is determined by the organizers based on its content.
Final rankings are determined by the total score earned by each participant.
In the case of a tie, the participant who answered correctly earlier will be ranked higher.
However, to prevent random guessing, the number of answer submissions is limited to 10 attempts per problem.
In addition, for each incorrect answer, a penalty of +10 minutes will be added to the time of the correct answer.

Participants may use only the following tools during the contest.

Participants must not engage in the following actions during the contest.

The use of the following is prohibited.

  • Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT
