Problem

There are \(64\) people, one at each point of a \(4\times4\times4\) lattice.

Each person has three arrows:

  • one parallel to the \(x\)-axis,
  • one parallel to the \(y\)-axis, and
  • one parallel to the \(z\)-axis.

Each arrow points in either the positive or negative direction of its axis.

Call an assignment of directions good if it satisfies both conditions:

  1. For every pair of adjacent people, exactly one of the two arrows parallel to the line joining them points toward the other person.
  2. For each person, exactly one of their three arrows points in a positive direction.

How many good assignments are there?
