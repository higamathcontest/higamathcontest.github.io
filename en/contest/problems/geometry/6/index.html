Problem

Triangle \(ABC\) satisfies

\[ AB=10,\qquad AC=17,\qquad BC=21. \]

Points \(P,Q\) lie on \(BC\), with

\[ PB=7,\qquad AQ\perp BC. \]

Let \(R\) be the second intersection, other than \(A\), of line \(AP\) with the circle through \(A,Q,B\).

Write

\[ AR=\frac{a\sqrt b}{c}, \]

where \(a,c\) are coprime positive integers and \(b\) is squarefree. Find \(a+b+c\).
